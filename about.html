I'm Sohan

I'm a first-year student at Franklin & Marshall College (class of 2030), looking at majoring in Business and minoring in Economics, with a growing interest in investing and wealth management.

I made this site because most finance information online jumps straight to stock picks, flashy screenshots or promises of getting rich. I wanted a simpler place to start that another student could actually read. I'm early in college, so I don't have every answer, and I tried to link a source for every number.

What I've done so far

  • Franklin & Marshall College, 2026 to 2030. First year, building a base in Business and Economics.
  • LaGuardia Community College, Business Services (July 2025–2026). Researched New York State electrical licensing requirements and built an interactive PDF guide.

What you will and won't find here

  • You will: Find plain-English lessons, real data with sources, and hypothetical investment calculators.
  • You won't: Find personal investment recommendations, promised returns, paid tips, or claims that any famous investor has a secret formula.
