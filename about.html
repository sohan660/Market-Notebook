About the creator

Hi, I'm Sohan Shrestha.

I'm a first-year student at Franklin & Marshall College. I'm interested in investing, business and wealth management, and I like projects where I can research something and make it easier for other people to use.

A little about me

Why I'm interested in finance

At Franklin & Marshall, I'm exploring Business, Organizations & Society and economics. I'm also taking calculus and getting more comfortable working with numbers. Wealth management interests me because it combines investments, research and conversations about people's long-term goals.

When I looked at the kind of investing content people share online, a lot of it focused on what to buy. This site focuses on the questions that come first: what does a stock represent, what does an ETF hold, and how can you tell whether a chart is useful?

I'm not presenting myself as an investment expert. I'm putting my research and notes in one place and linking the sources so other students can follow along.

Why the website exists

Why I made this guide

I wanted one place to send a classmate who asks how investing works. The goal isn't to recommend specific stocks. It's to show a beginner how to read a fund page, understand a fee and question a confident-sounding investing claim.

What you'll find here

Short explanations, charts with sources, examples you can test and a look at actual public filings.

What you won't find

Personalized portfolio recommendations, promises of returns, paid tips, or claims that one famous investor has a magic formula.
