THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECT

Hey, I'm Sohan.

I'm a first-year student at Franklin & Marshall College, interested in business, economics and wealth management.

Market Notebook started with a pretty simple thought: I kept finding investing advice online, but I wanted to understand the basics before having opinions about which stocks to buy.

So I built a place where I could turn what I was learning into short lessons, examples and small interactive tools. I'm still learning, and that's part of the point.

WHAT THIS SITE IS / ISN'T

No shortcuts to being a good investor.

You'll find plain-English explanations, hypothetical calculators, historical market data and tools for thinking through a company. You won't find guaranteed returns, a paid stock-picking service or personalized financial advice.

I care more about making finance feel approachable than pretending to know exactly where the market will go next.
