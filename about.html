Hi, I'm Sohan

I'm a first-year student at Franklin & Marshall College (class of 2030), looking at majoring in Business and minoring in Economics, with a growing interest in investing and wealth management.

I made this site because most finance information online jumps straight to stock picks, flashy screenshots or promises of getting rich. I wanted a simpler place to start that another student could actually read. I'm early in college, so I don't have every answer, and I tried to link a source for every number.

What I've done so far

  • Franklin & Marshall College, 2026 to 2030. First year, building a base in quantitative problem-solving and analysis.
  • LaGuardia Community College, Business Services (summer 2025). Researched New York State electrical licensing requirements and built an interactive PDF guide where readers pick a spot on a map to see which rules apply. Rules vary by municipality, so the guide points people to the right local authority. It taught me that accuracy matters more than speed.
  • IRS VITA/TCE certification. Part of learning practical financial skills. It doesn't mean I offer tax or financial advice through this site.
  • This site (2026). A beginner investing guide with charts, calculators and sources.

What you will and won't find here

You will: plain-English lessons, real data with sources, and hypothetical calculators.

You won't: personal recommendations, promised returns, paid tips, or a claim that any famous investor has a secret formula.
