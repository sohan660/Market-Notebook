# Your First Share — Beginner Investing Guide

**Your First Share** is a free, educational website built by Sohan Shrestha for college students who want to understand stocks, ETFs, investing habits, and lessons from well-known investors. This is **not investment advice**.

## Pages
- `index.html`: landing page
- `start.html`: beginner lessons about stocks, ETFs, brokerage accounts, risk, fees, and a simple checklist
- `investors.html`: sourced case studies (Buffett, Ackman, Soros, Bogle) and a **hypothetical** $100k-to-$1m calculator
- `about.html`: author background and LinkedIn contact

## Interactivity
`script.js` provides a hypothetical contribution-growth calculator, a $100k growth-to-$1m calculator, and a small knowledge quiz. No live stock-price feed or tracking. All assumptions are user-selectable and not forecasts.

## Sources and verification
The original educational summaries link to Investor.gov, Berkshire Hathaway's 2025 annual report, GuruFocus's public investor profiles (snapshot information, not live holdings), the SEC's Form 13F FAQ, and Vanguard's Bogle/indexing history.

**Important:** GuruFocus-reported 13F portfolio values refer to disclosed securities holdings, not a guru's personal gains or verified total fund returns. A 13F can omit assets and may be filed up to 45 days after quarter-end. Historical investment performance does not guarantee future results.
